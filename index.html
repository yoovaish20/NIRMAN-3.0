How FixMyCampus Works

A streamlined, 3-step workflow connecting student complaints to rapid facility resolution.

1

1. Report

Students submit a campus problem with a concise description, category, specific room/floor location, and optional photo upload.

2

2. Prioritize

The AI engine instantly assesses linguistic hazard markers, assigns a 0–100 urgency score, and flags duplicates across campus.

3

3. Resolve

Administrators auto-assign tasks to maintenance, IT, or plumbing teams and track progress until complete resolution.
